# My Bible

A quiet, distraction free reading environment for the Holy Scriptures.

## Purpose

Reading Scripture should feel peaceful, patient, and unhurried. Many digital Bible apps surround the reader with excessive navigation menus, floating buttons, social feeds, and visual noise. My Bible strips away unnecessary elements so you can focus entirely on the text itself.

## Philosophy

Every detail in this reading room is designed with uncommon care and restraint.

* Pure typography centered on legible editorial typefaces.
* Generous whitespace that allows passages to breathe naturally on the page.
* Calm light and dark modes designed to remain gentle on the eyes during morning devotionals and evening study alike.
* Support for multiple translations so you can reflect on scripture from different perspectives.
* Immediate access to every book, chapter, and verse without clutter.
